Research Fellow
Tel-Hai, The University of Kiryat Shmona in the Galilee

I'm currently a Research Fellow at Tel-Hai, The University of Kiryat Shmona in the Galilee. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Multi-Robot Systems (MRS) Lab, Technion. Prior to that, I was a one-year Postdoctoral Fellow in the Autonomous Navigation and Perception Lab (ANPL) at Technion. I received a B.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering from the School of Electrical Engineering at Tel Aviv University, Israel, in 2014, an M.Sc. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Technion in 2018, and a Ph.D. degree from the Technion Autonomous Systems Program (TASP) in 2024. My current research interests include Algorithmic Probabilistic Robotics, Explainable Physical AI, and Human-in-the-Loop Robotics.
